Getting Started — New User Setup

Follow these steps in order the first time you set up the app.

1
Add Raw Stock items
Go to Raw Stock and add every ingredient or material your products consume (e.g. water, syrup, cups). Give each item a name, unit, and starting quantity.
2
Add Vendors
Go to Vendors and add the suppliers you buy stock from. You need at least one vendor before you can record a restock.
3
Add Purchase products
Go to Products and add the items you buy from vendors. Set the type to Purchase. These will be available when recording a restock.
4
Record your first Restock
Go to Restock, select your vendor, add your purchase products with quantities and unit costs, then save. This logs the expense and updates your stock levels.
5
Add Sale products
Go to Products and add what you sell to customers. Set the type to Sale and enable Show in POS. Link raw stock items as ingredients so stock is deducted automatically on each sale.
6
Make your first sale
Go to POS, tap your products to build a cart, choose a payment method (Cash or GCash), and click Charge. The transaction and stock deductions are recorded automatically.
7
Update your Settings
Go to Settings to set the app title and change your username or password. Superadmins can also add or manage other user accounts here.
POS — Point of Sale

The POS screen is where you record sales to customers.

Making a sale
  1. Navigate to POS in the sidebar.
  2. Click any product button to add it to the cart. Use the + / − buttons or type a quantity directly.
  3. Optionally search for and select a Customer using the customer search box at the top.
  4. Choose a payment method: Cash or GCash.
  5. For cash payments, enter the amount tendered — change due is calculated automatically.
  6. Click Charge to complete the sale. A receipt summary is shown and the transaction is recorded.
Notes
  • Only products marked Show in POS and of type Sale appear on the POS screen.
  • Stock is deducted automatically for products that have linked ingredients.
Products

Products are the items your business sells or purchases. Each product has a type that controls where it appears.

Product types
TypeDescription
SaleItems sold to customers at the POS. Can be shown or hidden on the POS screen.
PurchaseItems bought from vendors for restocking. Never shown on the POS screen.
Adding a product
  1. Go to Products and click Add Product.
  2. Enter the name, price, and unit (e.g. cup, bottle).
  3. Select the product type: Sale or Purchase.
  4. Optionally upload a photo (JPEG/PNG, max 2 MB).
  5. Click Save.
Show in POS toggle

On the Products list, use the toggle switch in the POS column to show or hide a Sale-type product on the POS screen. Purchase products cannot be toggled.

Customers

Keep a record of your regular customers to attach them to transactions.

Adding a customer
  1. Go to Customers and click Add Customer.
  2. Enter the customer's name and optionally their phone number.
  3. Click Save.
Editing / deleting

Click the pencil icon next to a customer to edit their details. Click the trash icon to delete. A customer with existing transactions cannot be deleted.

Vendors

Vendors are suppliers you buy stock from. Every purchase restock is linked to a vendor.

Adding a vendor
  1. Go to Vendors and click Add Vendor.
  2. Enter the vendor name and optionally contact details.
  3. Click Save.
Editing / deleting

Use the action buttons on each vendor row. A vendor linked to existing restock records cannot be deleted.

Restock

Record purchases from vendors to replenish your inventory.

Recording a restock
  1. Go to Restock and click New Restock.
  2. Select the vendor you purchased from.
  3. Add one or more Purchase-type products, entering the quantity and unit cost for each.
  4. Click Save — the cost is recorded as an expense and stock is updated automatically.
Notes

Only Purchase-type products are available for restocking. The total cost of each restock is recorded as a transaction expense in the ledger.

Raw Stock

Raw Stock tracks ingredients or materials used by your sale products.

Stock items
  1. Go to Raw Stock to see all stock items and their current quantities.
  2. Add new stock items with a name, unit, and starting quantity.
  3. Link stock items to sale products as ingredients — when a sale is made, the ingredient quantities are deducted automatically.
Manual adjustment

You can manually increase or decrease stock levels using the adjustment controls on each stock item row.

Transactions

The Transactions page shows a full history of all sales and expense records.

Using the list
  • Use the search bar to find transactions by ID, customer name, or notes.
  • Use the Type and Status filters to narrow results.
  • Click any column header to sort.
  • Use the rows per page selector to control how many records are shown.
Transaction types
TypeDescription
SaleRevenue from a customer purchase at the POS.
ExpenseCost recorded when a restock is saved.
Settings
Change your credentials
  1. Go to Settings.
  2. Enter a new username and/or new password in the Change Credentials form.
  3. Confirm the new password and click Save.

Finance Superadmin

The Finance page gives a high-level view of revenue versus expenses.

  • Summary cards — total revenue, total expenses, and net profit for the selected period.
  • Sales ledger — list of all sale transactions with amounts and payment methods.
  • Expenses ledger — list of all restock expenses with vendor and product details.
Monitoring Superadmin

Monitoring shows live system health and a full audit log of all user actions.

  • Live stats — active sessions, request counts, and error rates updated every few seconds via a live stream.
  • Audit log — every login, logout, create, update, and delete action is recorded with the username, timestamp, and details.
  • Security events — failed login attempts and permission-denied errors are flagged separately.
